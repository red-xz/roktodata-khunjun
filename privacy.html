Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026  •  Developer: SOHEL  •  Contact: mustak105@gmail.com

1. What data we collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us when you register, and only what the app needs to work:

  • Donor registration: name, phone number, address, age, gender, blood group, profession, last donation date (optional), photo (optional).
  • Hospital registration: name, phone number, address, facilities, doctors practising there, photo, and a licence/approval document photo.
  • Doctor registration: name, phone number, degree, specialty, workplace, government/private, medical registration number (optional), address, photo.
  • Location: only when you tap “Use my location” or “Live location” — your GPS position, or the place name you type, so we can show donors near you.
  • Reviews and reports: your name, star rating, review text, and reports of listings you flag.
  • Technical: an anonymous account ID (Firebase Anonymous Auth) and a random owner token stored on your own device, so only you can edit or delete your listings. We do not collect your device identifiers, contacts, messages, or advertising IDs.
  • Account (optional): if you create an account, your phone number is your login ID. Your password is stored hashed by Firebase Authentication — we never see your password.

2. How we use your data

  • To show your listing to people searching for blood, hospitals, or doctors near them.
  • To sort results by distance and blood-group compatibility.
  • To let you manage (edit, pause, delete) your own listings from the device you registered with.
  • To show your licence status (“licence provided”) on hospital listings. Your licence photo itself is private — only you can see it.
  • To moderate reports: a listing reported by 3 or more users is hidden automatically.

We never sell your data. We show no ads and use no advertising SDKs.

3. What is public — and what is never shown

When you register, you agree that your name, phone number, blood group, area/district, profession, and photo are visible to other users of the app — that is how people in need of blood can call you. Your street address and exact GPS coordinates are never shown in search results; only the distance (for example “2.3 km”) is shown. Your licence document photo is never public.

4. Public directory listings

Some donors, hospitals, and doctors in the app come from public directories where they published their own details for blood donation. These are marked “Public listing / not yet verified” with a source link. If your details appear and you want them removed, use “Remove my information” in the app (Profile tab) with your phone number, or email us — we will remove them.

5. Who we share data with

  • Google Firebase (Firestore + Authentication): stores the listings and serves the app. Data is stored on Google’s servers (Asia-Southeast region). See Google’s privacy policy for how Google handles it.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim: when you search a place name or ask for nearby hospitals, the place text is sent to Nominatim to find coordinates. No personal data is sent.

We share nothing else with anyone, and we do not transfer data for advertising.

6. Location

Location is used only when you ask for it (nearby search or live location at registration). We never track your location in the background. You can use the app without location by typing a district name instead.

7. How long we keep data — and how to delete it

Your listing stays until you delete it. You can delete your data at any time:

  • In the app: Profile tab → your listing → Delete. This removes it from the app immediately.
  • Public listing removal: Profile tab → “Remove my information” with your phone number.
  • By email: write to mustak105@gmail.com with your name and phone number, and we will delete your data.

Deleting a listing removes it from the live database. Backups kept by Google Firebase are deleted on Google’s schedule.

8. Children

The app is for adults aged 18 and over only (blood donors must be 18–70). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child’s data is in the app, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Only the owner of a listing (verified by their anonymous account) can edit or delete it. Licence documents are stored in a private area that only the owner can read.

10. Medical disclaimer

This app does not provide medical advice. It only helps people find each other. Whether someone can donate blood is decided by medical professionals at the time of donation.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and note the change in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept it.

12. Contact

Questions, corrections, or deletion requests: mustak105@gmail.com.
